Gravity Pixel Font Pack
=======================

Source: https://jotson.itch.io/gravity-pixel-font

This directory vendors the two TTF files used to render the Tumoflip post-update
splash screens:

- GravityBold8.ttf
- GravityRegular5.ttf

The upstream page describes the pack as usable in commercial and non-commercial
projects. Keep the fonts at their pixel-perfect sizes: multiples of 8 for Bold
and multiples of 5 for Regular.
